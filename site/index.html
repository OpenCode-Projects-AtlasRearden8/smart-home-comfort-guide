Why this guide

Smart devices are tools: they listen, switch, warm, and watch, in service of small everyday comforts. Each one has senses and habits worth knowing about, which is exactly what this manual explains — calmly, once, and in order. When you are ready to act, the room-by-room checklist and the privacy settings table turn the reading into twenty tidy minutes.
