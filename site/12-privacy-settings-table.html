One table, meant to be checked against your actual devices. Open each companion app, visit each named page once, and make your choices deliberately — then stop worrying about it. Most settings here are set-once-and-forget.

Names differ slightly by brand ("Data & Privacy," "Voice History," "Account settings"); the kind of setting is the same everywhere, which is what the table tracks.

Print-friendly summary card

The 6-minute privacy pass (new device in the house):

  1. Name it something you'd say aloud.
  2. Voice history / recordings: off or short auto-delete.
  3. Audio recording off on cameras (if unused).
  4. Local storage if offered; cloud only by conscious choice.
  5. Two-factor authentication on the account.
  6. Guest network for anything belonging to visitors.

Do this once per device, then go back to drinking tea.
